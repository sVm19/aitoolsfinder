# Admin and Content Operations

## 1. Admin responsibilities
- Create and update tools.
- Manage categories, tags and features.
- Review vendor submissions.
- Verify official websites and listing information.
- Manage pricing plans and source links.
- Publish or archive content.
- Review audit logs and data freshness.

## 2. Tool publication checklist
Before publishing:
- Confirm product name and official website.
- Confirm the destination URL is safe and belongs to the product.
- Write a factual, original description.
- Assign at least one appropriate category.
- Add relevant tags.
- Record pricing only with a source and verification date.
- Record feature claims with evidence where practical.
- Check logo/media rights and source.
- Ensure slug uniqueness.
- Review title, description and canonical URL.
- Label sponsorship where applicable.

## 3. Data freshness
Store `last_verified_at` for tool and pricing information. Define a review cadence based on importance and volatility. Display uncertainty when data is old or unavailable.

## 4. Moderation
Submissions begin in pending status. Admin approval is required before publication. Rejections should record an internal reason. Prevent vendors from editing another vendor's submissions.

## 5. Editorial integrity
- Sponsored placements are labelled.
- Affiliate relationships are disclosed.
- Paid placement does not guarantee an organic ranking.
- Reviews and ratings must be authentic and moderated under a published policy.
- Never fabricate testimonials, traffic, revenue or benchmark data.
